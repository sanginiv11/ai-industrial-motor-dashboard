V3 Randomized Ensemble - Very Hard Independent Test
===================================================

Architecture:
- 164 engineered features per 256-sample window
- Time-domain robust statistics
- Cross-sensor ratios
- FFT band energies
- RPM-normalized 0.5x..6x harmonic energies
- Harmonic energy ratios
- Spectral centroid/entropy
- ExtraTrees + XGBoost probability ensemble

Training:
- 1,500 generated randomized training windows (250/class)
- Training parameter distribution differs from hard-test parameter distribution
- No hard-test windows used in training

VERY HARD HOLDOUT:
- 4 unseen seeds: 91427, 381991, 702114, 111223
- 100 windows/class/seed
- 2,400 total windows
- Test RPM range 900-2100 RPM
- Wider load, voltage, calibration, noise and drift variation
- Overlapping fault severities
- Fault frequencies vary with RPM
